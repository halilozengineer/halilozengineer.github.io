Introduction

In motorcycles, the drive chain is the most critical transmission element delivering engine power to the rear wheel. Yet due to mechanical friction and thermal cycling, chains inevitably elongate over time and lose optimal tension. A slack chain creates life-threatening risks of derailment, snapping, and rear-wheel lockup, whereas an over-tightened chain destroys transmission output bearings and sprockets.

This project covers the design, FEA verification, and R&D workflow of LubriTens—an electromechanical plug-and-play system that eliminates periodic manual chain adjustment and lubrication by monitoring tension in real time while riding and applying precision micro-dosed lubrication.

Engineering & Learning Objectives

  • Measure micron-level chain elongation in dynamic riding environments using Strain Gauge sensors while filtering out road vibration noise.
  • Design a low-friction bearing-mounted idler sprocket and actuator kinematic architecture instead of conventional friction slider blocks.
  • Develop a universal adjustable swingarm clamp that provides rigid mounting without welding or drilling into the motorcycle chassis.
  • Validate housing durability and modal natural frequencies in ANSYS Workbench against road impacts, engine resonance, and harsh weather (IP67).

Project Summary

LubriTens is an integrated mechatronic product consisting of a compact control housing clamped onto the rear swingarm, strain gauge load sensors, an ECU-controlled linear actuator, a bearing-mounted idler sprocket, and a micro-dosing oil pump. Unlike passive vacuum oilers on the market (such as Scottoiler), LubriTens not only lubricates the chain but actively regulates chain tension via closed-loop feedback—reducing oil waste and chemical runoff by 60% while doubling (2x) the service life of the chain and sprocket set.

Problem Definition

Motorcycle drive chain maintenance presents three major bottlenecks across rider safety, fleet operating costs, and environmental impact:

  • Critical Safety Hazards: Manual chain tensioning requires mechanical precision. A loose chain jumping off the rear sprocket can instantly lock the rear wheel, while over-tensioning fractures countershaft bearings.
  • Premature Wear & High Maintenance Cost: A chain-and-sprocket set that is not regularly lubricated and tensioned wears out in half its nominal lifespan. For commercial courier fleets, vehicle downtime represents a substantial financial loss.
  • Environmental Contamination: During manual aerosol spray lubrication, more than 40% of the lubricant flings off onto the tire rim and road surface.

Approach & Design Process

To overcome the limitations of passive friction blocks and vacuum drippers, an active mechatronic architecture was engineered:

  1. Weld-Free Universal Swingarm Clamp: A two-piece bolted compression clamp was designed to fit standard rear swingarm profiles without compromising chassis structural integrity.
  2. Idler Sprocket & Dynamic Tensioning Kinematics: A ball-bearing idler sprocket mounted on the actuator shaft engages the lower chain run, applying millimeter-precise vertical tension without inducing parasitic drag.
  3. Strain Gauge Smart Sensing: Reaction forces exerted by the chain are measured via strain gauges. An embedded ECU filtering algorithm distinguishes transient road bumps from permanent chain slack before triggering the actuator.
  4. 5-Stage Smart Operating Modes:
    • Mode 1 (Monitoring): Sensors scan chain load in standby at millisecond intervals.
    • Mode 2 (Auto-Tensioning): When slack exceeds the threshold, the actuator advances the idler sprocket to restore target tension.
    • Mode 3 (Periodic Lubrication): At calibrated distance/time intervals, the micro-pump delivers a precise oil pulse onto the chain rollers.
    • Mode 4 (Manual Boost): A handlebar button allows the rider to trigger extra lubrication in heavy rain or dusty off-road conditions.
    • Mode 5 (Service Mode): During tire or chain replacement, the actuator fully retracts and locks the piston.
  5. Modal Vibration Verification: Modal analyses in ANSYS Workbench verified that the natural frequency modes of the assembly remain safely above engine excitation frequencies, preventing resonance fatigue.

Results & Outputs

Technical and commercial performance indicators validated under the TÜBİTAK 1812 business plan and engineering simulations:

  • 2x Extended Chain Lifespan: Keeping the chain continuously at optimal tension and micro-lubricated cuts wear rates in half and prevents sprocket tooth stripping.
  • 60% Lubricant Savings & Eco-Protection: Targeted pulse dosing eliminates the uncontrolled fling-off of manual aerosol sprays.
  • Strong Competitive Edge: Unlike imported passive oilers that only lubricate, LubriTens combines active tensioning and lubrication in a single unit with a 30%–40% cost advantage.
  • Rapid Break-Even Point: Financial modeling confirms a break-even threshold at just 93 units sold.

Summary

This study delivers a domestically developed, patent-worthy mechatronic solution supported by TÜBİTAK 1812 BİGG R&D that directly addresses motorcycle chain slack and dry-running hazards.

Key Takeaways

  • Hybrid Mechatronic Engineering: Idler sprocket kinematics, embedded ECU filtering, and strain gauge force sensing were seamlessly integrated.
  • Enhanced Riding Safety: Chain derailment and rear-wheel lockup risks are eliminated without requiring manual rider intervention.
  • Scalable Manufacturing Roadmap: A clear transition from CNC-machined prototypes to die-cast aluminum housings and custom PCBs was established.
